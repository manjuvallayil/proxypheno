Proof of concept

Every routine measurement is a potential new phenotype.

ProxyPheno learns the joint distribution of routine dairy phenotypes, scores how unexpected each animal's profile is, and turns coherent outliers into candidate phenotypes for breeding and animal-health teams.

The problem

The traits that matter most - health, resilience, welfare, emissions - are expensive or impossible to measure at population scale. Meanwhile, LIC already generates millions of routine measurements. The gap is not data volume; it is turning routine signal into traits that can be acted on.

Approach

Conditional residuals

For each trait, predict it from the rest of the animal's profile and keep the residual. This is the proxy-phenotype signal.

Novelty scoring

PCA-space Mahalanobis distance plus IsolationForest, combined into a calibrated percentile score.

Signature groups

HDBSCAN found no density-separated clusters, so animals are grouped by their dominant residual trait and direction. That is honest and interpretable.

Honest baseline comparison

Ridge, LightGBM and an MLP use identical splits. The linear baseline is strong; ML adds a modest but consistent gain.

Known-locus validation

Test whether signatures and trait tails enrich for carriers of the paper's prioritised recessive mutations, with Benjamini-Hochberg control.

Grounded translation

Precomputed briefs narrate structured results. Numbers are injected, never generated.

Results

Residual phenotype space. Each point is an animal; colour is the novelty score. The space is a single continuum, not discrete clusters.
Positive control: PLCD4 carriers weigh 25.9% less than controls (147.1 vs 198.4 kg; 95% CI -62.3 to -39.4 kg), matching the published effect.
Dominant-trait signatures. Each animal is labelled by the trait with the largest conditional residual (|z| >= 3), or diffuse.
Known-locus recovery: the chr14 casein region is strongly enriched in milk and protein tails (q < 1e-100), including within breed.
Proxy-prediction on an identical 80/20 split: LightGBM mean R² 0.488, MLP 0.481, Ridge 0.479. LightGBM wins on 14 of 16 traits, but the linear baseline is already strong.

Discovery-support, not diagnosis. The pipeline ranks hypotheses for the genetics and animal-health teams; it does not establish causality.

Built on published science

The anchor is Reynolds et al. (2021), Non-additive association analysis using proxy phenotypes identifies novel cattle syndromes, Nature Genetics 53, 949-954. The POC extends that proxy-phenotype idea with a modern ML and translation layer.

Data: https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.kwh70rz1z. Code and results are reproducible from the public repository.
